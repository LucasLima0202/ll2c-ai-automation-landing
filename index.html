What we automate

  • Lead follow-up

    Forms, email, WhatsApp, CRM

    An AI assistant answers common questions from your own documents and passes the rest to your team with the full context.

    Website chat, email, help desk

  • Documents and data entry

    PDFs, scans, ERP, spreadsheets

    Your weekly numbers are pulled from every tool and delivered to your inbox, without anyone copying and pasting.

    Sheets, dashboards, Slack

    E-signature, Drive, calendar

    For work that is specific to your business, we build agents that handle multi-step tasks and ask a person before anything important.

Questions we hear often

Do we need to change the software we use? +

No. We connect the tools you already have. If something is missing, we'll recommend the simplest option and explain why.

Is our data safe? +

Your data stays in your accounts. Automations only get the access they need, and we document exactly what each one can see and do.

What happens when the AI isn't sure? +

It hands the task to a person on your team, with everything it found so far. You decide which actions always need a human to approve.

How much does it cost? +

Each project has a fixed price, agreed after the audit, plus an optional monthly plan for maintenance. You'll know the cost and the expected hours saved before we start.

How long until we see results? +

Most first automations are live within a month, and you see hours saved from the first week they run.
